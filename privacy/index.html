Privacy Policy

📝 Draft — subject to legal review.

Effective date: 18 September 2026

App: Spendly (“Spendly”, “the app”, “we”, “us”)

Contact: hello@spendlyapp.me

1. Who we are and what Spendly is

Spendly is a personal finance tracking and AI money-coaching app. It helps you record expenses, see what’s safe to spend, set budgets, and get general money-habit guidance.

Spendly is not a bank, lender, broker, or licensed financial advisor. It does not hold your money, connect to your bank accounts, or move funds. Guidance in the app is educational and general in nature only (see Section 9).

2. Our privacy approach: local-first

Spendly is local-first. By default, your data is stored on your device and stays there. Cloud sync is optional and only happens if you choose to create an account and sign in (see Section 4). You can use Spendly without an account.

3. What data we collect and where it lives

a) Data you create in the app (stored locally on your device)

  • Expenses you add: amount, merchant/description, category, date, source label, and optional note.
  • Settings and preferences: display currency, monthly budget, savings goal, per-category budgets, AI-Coach on/off, dark mode.
  • Learned merchant-to-category preferences (e.g. that you file a given merchant under a given category), stored only on your device.
  • AI chat messages you type in the coach screen and their replies. These are stored on your device. If you have turned on AI data sharing, the message you send — together with a few of your most recent messages and replies from the same conversation — is also transmitted for processing as described in Section 6.
  • App state: whether you’ve completed onboarding, and your local Pro-trial toggle status.

This data remains on your device unless you enable cloud sync.

b) Account data (only if you create an account)

If you choose to sign in, we use [Supabase] (our hosting and authentication provider) to create an account. We collect your email address, an authentication credential (password) handled by the auth provider, and a system-generated user ID. We ask for minimal personal information — an email is all that’s required. We do not require your name, phone number, address, or government ID.

c) Data synced to the cloud (only if you sign in)

If you’re signed in, the following is synced to your account so you can back it up and use it across devices:

  • your expenses,
  • your profile settings (currency, monthly budget, savings goal, AI-Coach preference), and
  • your category budgets.

Not synced (kept on-device only): dark-mode preference, onboarding status, learned merchant-to-category preferences, AI usage counters, local Pro-trial status, your AI data-sharing choice, and AI chat history. Your chat history is never uploaded to your account — note that your message and a few recent messages from the same conversation are still sent for AI processing as described in Section 6, but they are not stored there.

d) Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not connect to your bank, read your real SMS messages, or access your transactions automatically. Any “Auto-Track”, receipt-scan, or voice features currently in the app are simulated demonstrations (see Section 6).
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children (see Section 12).

4. Accounts and authentication

  • Accounts are optional. Without one, Spendly runs fully locally.
  • Authentication is provided by [Supabase]. Your credentials are handled by that provider; we only ever use a public “anon” key in the app, and each account can only access its own data via database row-level security.
  • Signing out ends your cloud session but keeps your data on your device.

5. Payments and subscriptions

  • Spendly offers an optional paid “Pro” subscription.
  • We do not process or store your payment details — no card number ever reaches us. Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store and Google Play, and managed through our subscription provider [RevenueCat], under their own privacy policies.
  • To link a purchase to your account, we send RevenueCat your Spendly account ID (never your email or your name). From them we receive your subscription status — whether Pro is active, when it renews or expires, and whether a free trial has been used. We use this only to unlock what you paid for.

6. AI features

Spendly’s AI money coach and AI expense parsing send the message you type, together with a summary of your budget numbers, to Spendly’s own backend service, which passes it to our AI provider, OpenAI, to generate a reply. OpenAI processes the request in the United States.

What is sent

  • the message you type;
  • so the coach can follow the conversation, up to your last 8 messages and replies from the past two hours, each trimmed to a short length;
  • a numbers-only summary — your currency, safe-to-spend, month and week totals, monthly budget, budget left, savings goal, subscriptions total, biggest spending category, and Money Age.

What is never sent

  • your email address, your name, your password, your account ID, your device identifiers, your location, your list of expenses, or any older messages — we never send your full chat history.

Your permission

We ask you first. The first time you use Spendly AI we show you what is sent and you choose Continue or Not now. Nothing is sent to our backend or our AI provider until you choose Continue. If you choose Not now, the rest of Spendly works normally.

Changing your mind

Turn AI data sharing off in Settings → Privacy & Permissions at any time. Future AI requests stop immediately. The conversation already on your phone stays there until you clear it — there is a Clear AI chat history button on the same screen, and it does not touch your expenses. If we materially change what is sent, we will ask you again.

How you are identified

You are not. We send a one-way scrambled code derived from your account ID so that our provider can enforce their abuse limits. It cannot be turned back into your identity, and your email address is never involved.

Why

Solely to generate the coaching reply or the expense suggestion you asked for. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to build a profile of you.

Retention

Spendly does not store your AI messages on our server. We keep only anonymous counters — how many requests were made, how long they took, and how many tokens were used — which contain no message content and are not tied to you. Your chat history stays on your device. We ask our AI provider not to retain these requests; they may still keep them for a limited period for their own abuse monitoring, and they handle data under their own API terms and privacy policy.

Still simulated

Voice entry, receipt scanning (OCR), and Auto-Track remain simulated placeholders and do not use your microphone, camera, or real SMS or bank data. We will update this policy before any of those becomes real.

AI output is general educational guidance, not personalized financial, legal, or tax advice (see Section 9), and it can be inaccurate — always check it yourself.

7. How we use data

We use your data to run the app’s core features (tracking, budgets, safe-to-spend, Money Age), provide optional backup and sync, operate accounts, generate AI coaching replies and expense suggestions when you use the AI Coach (see Section 6), and process subscriptions via the providers in Section 5. We do not use your financial data for advertising and do not sell it.

8. Sharing and third parties

We share data only with service providers strictly needed to run features you use:

  • [Supabase] — account/authentication and cloud sync (only if you sign in).
  • [Apple] / [Google] / [RevenueCat] — subscription processing (only if and when you subscribe).
  • [OpenAI] — AI coaching and expense parsing (only when you use the AI Coach, and only after you have agreed). Your message, a little of your recent conversation, and a numbers-only budget summary are processed by OpenAI in the United States under their API terms.
  • [Sentry] — crash and error reporting. Crash reports carry the error and your device/OS/app version. They are stripped of anything you typed, your email, and every financial value, and are not linked to your account.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy. We do not otherwise share, rent, or sell your data.

9. No financial, legal, or tax advice

Spendly provides budgeting tools and general educational guidance only. It is not a licensed financial, investment, legal, or tax advisor, and nothing in the app is personalized professional advice. You are responsible for your own financial decisions; consider a qualified professional for advice specific to your situation.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • Local data: stays on your device until you delete it. Use Settings → Delete transaction data to clear expenses on the device, or uninstall the app to remove all local data.
  • Cloud data (if you have an account): you can delete your on-device transaction data (expenses) in the app at any time (via Delete transaction data); your budgets and settings remain on the device. You can delete your whole account and its cloud data from inside the app, at Settings → Account → Delete my account. You will be asked to confirm who you are first. This removes your account and the data synced to it, signs you out, and clears the local data on this device. You can also email hello@spendlyapp.me; we process verified requests within 30 days. Where law requires limited retention, we keep only what it requires.
  • You can export your expenses to a CSV file from within the app at any time.

11. Data security

  • Data in transit to our providers is protected with encryption (HTTPS); providers encrypt data at rest.
  • Only a public key ships in the app; access to your account data is restricted to your own account by row-level security. No secret keys are embedded in the app.
  • No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Children’s privacy

Spendly is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise them, contact hello@spendlyapp.me. We may need to verify your identity first.

14. International users

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own (e.g. where our providers host data). Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards. Data-hosting region: ap-northeast-1 (Tokyo) for your account and synced data (Supabase). When you use the AI Coach, your message and a numbers-only budget summary are also processed by OpenAI in the United States.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date, and where required we’ll seek your consent before new data uses take effect.
